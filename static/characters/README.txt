NOTE: the app now shows its own front-facing cartoon tutors by default (they look straight at you, move their
mouth with the voice, blink, nod and gesture with their hands).
Files/folders in here OVERRIDE them. If you unzipped picture puppets (yuri/ or joohyuk/ folders, yuri.png...)
and want the front-facing drawn tutors back, just delete those folders/files.

Put your own tutor artwork here to replace the built-in cartoon faces:

  yuri.png      (the girl tutor)
  joohyuk.png   (the boy tutor)

Tips:
- Best: a PNG with a TRANSPARENT background (a "cut-out"), character facing front, head and shoulders,
  with the bottom edge faded out. She/he stands in the savanna scene inside the video box.
- File names must be exactly yuri / joohyuk (lowercase).
- Delete a file to go back to the built-in drawing for that tutor.
- These files are NOT uploaded to GitHub (see .gitignore): artwork stays on your computer.
- Only use artwork you have permission to use, and credit the creator in your project report.

PUPPET MODE (smoothest, hands visible):
  Put the full-body artwork cut into pieces in a folder named after the tutor, for example static/characters/yuri/
  with body.png, armL.png, armR.png, head.png, full.png (all the same size) and a rig.json that lists them
  with their pivot points. The pieces move separately: bounce, nodding head, waving arms, breathing.
